No. 04 · Process4 steps
01

Full analysis

Before we produce any content, we fully analyse our clients' social media channels to see what we're working with.

02

Strategy building

Once we have all the key information, we build a short-term and then a long-term strategy, so we're never just posting aimlessly.

03

On-location shoot

At a time and place agreed with the client in advance, we shoot the material the strategy calls for.

04

Post-production, posting, reporting

From the footage we create high-quality videos, photos and posts, which we prepare and publish according to the strategy, then analyse and report on.

No. 05 · FAQ01 — 05
01What costs should I expect?
We put together a fully tailored quote for every potential client.
02How long does a shoot take?
It varies from project to project and depends on many factors, but on average 3–4 hours.
03Why should I choose you?
We don't aim to be one of many. We know there's something special about us that other companies can't offer: a unique value that others won't be able to copy. We are the agency that doesn't just make videos and posts for businesses, but brings out emotions and moods.
04Do I have to appear in the videos?
No! Someone else from inside the company can do the job perfectly well. But we know people like to connect with a person, so it's usually the company's owners who appear in the videos.
05Can you take on post-production only?
Of course! In fact, these projects are usually quicker, and we take them on at a lower price.
