7. Your rights

In short: you can ask what we know about you, have it corrected or deleted, and withdraw your consent at any time.

  • Access: ask which of your data we process and get a copy (GDPR Art. 15).
  • Rectification: have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16).
  • Erasure: have your data deleted when it is no longer needed or you have withdrawn consent (Art. 17).
  • Restriction: ask us to only store your data for a while (Art. 18).
  • Portability: receive the data you gave us in a machine-readable format (Art. 20).
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21).
  • Withdrawing consent: at any time, with "Cookie settings" or by email (Art. 7(3)).

Send your request to hello@7sma.hu. We reply within one month at the latest (extendable by two further months where justified; we will tell you if so). It is free of charge.
